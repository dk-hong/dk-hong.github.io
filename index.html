AI ENGINEER & RESEARCHER

Deokki Hong.

Research in efficient models.Engineering for practical systems.

I am an AI engineer on the Software SDK team at Rebellions. My current interests include model compression and inference optimization for large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs), with a focus on making these models more efficient to deploy and run.

My research background spans data-free quantization and differentiable neural architecture–accelerator co-exploration. I received my master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from Yonsei University, where I worked with Prof. Jinho Lee in the AcSys lab.
